Flying

  • ←→ turn, ↑↓ nose up and down (or WS).
  • Hold Space to fly. Add Shift for warp speed, about five times faster.
  • L locks the controls so you fly in a perfectly straight line.
  • H takes you back home and clears your measurements.

Measuring

  • Beacons (B): drop three. Laser beams, the straightest possible lines, join them into a triangle and its angles are measured.
  • Parallel torpedoes (P): two torpedoes fly off side by side, pointing the same way. Watch whether the separation between them stays the same, shrinks or grows.
  • Your path (T) is traced for the last minute. Fly straight and see if you come back home.
  • Landmarks: home is the Earth. A gold-and-magenta nebula, a big teal spiral and an edge-on galaxy are one of a kind. See one twice and light has gone all the way around the universe.

The minimap

The minimap is the 2D analogy of your universe's shape: a plane, a torus, a sphere or a saddle. It follows two of your three directions. Drag it to spin it. In a mystery universe it stays hidden until you check your guess.

The ship and torpedoes are drawn far larger than true scale.
